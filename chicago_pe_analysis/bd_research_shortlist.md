# Research shortlist for SEI Chicago: ten companies, five PE firms

A **neutral research shortlist, not a ranking**. The workbook has no evidence of budget, buying intent, financial trouble or an open consulting project at any company, so nothing here is scored. Companies are grouped by the kind of dated fact the workbook (or a web check) supports, and listed alphabetically within each group.

How to read each entry
- **Workbook facts**: what the workbook says, with row IDs and its source URLs (taken from the `HYPERLINK` formulas).
- **Web check (2026-10-19)**: what I found by search on that date. Page fetches failed for some hosts, so these checks rest on search results and the sources they returned; a "not found" is not evidence that nothing happened. Announcement and completion dates are kept separate.
- **Open questions**: what is not established.
- **Consulting hypothesis**: my interpretation, **not evidence**. It names a theme that *could* be relevant and what would confirm or rule it out.

Selection rule: Chicago-area location in the workbook, plus a dated ownership or transaction fact, plus ownership evidence that is better than a bare listing. Industry fit alone was not enough. Companies that are public, are SEI peers, or have only an undated listing were left out (see the end).

## D. Long hold with reported financing stress

### Cision (C016), Chicago
- **Workbook facts:** Platinum Equity take-private, acquisition Jan 2020; "Active" on Platinum's page. High, undated. Source: https://www.platinumequity.com/our-company/cision/
- **Web check:** Completion of the 2020 acquisition: https://www.platinumequity.com/news/platinum-equity-completes-2-7-billion-acquisition-of-cision/. Bloomberg Law coverage (search summary dates it 2025; exact date unverified) reports that talks with lenders broke down and that term loans due 2027 traded at distressed levels: https://news.bloomberglaw.com/bankruptcy-law/americas-distress-daily-cision-debt-talks-have-broken-down. I found no 2026 sale, refinancing or ownership change; the lack of one is not proof.
- **Open questions:** Whether creditors, not Platinum, will control future decisions; the current debt position.
- **Consulting hypothesis:** A company under financing pressure *may* look at cost and operating-model work, but a restructuring more often brings turnaround advisers and lender-led choices. Treat as a research item with a high chance of ownership change before any engagement.
