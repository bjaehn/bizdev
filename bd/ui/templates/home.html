One press does all of this, in order, and stops at the spending limit. Signals come first; facts and new companies get what is left:

  1. Reads the SEC's own data for public companies (free), so the filing scanners know which company is which.
  2. Reads each private equity firm's own portfolio page (free), and looks on each company's own careers page for its job board, so its postings come from the free complete feed (free).
  3. Reads every company's own job board (open roles, and the programs its job titles name) and its SEC event filings: 8-K items such as officer changes, restructuring, cyber incidents, defaults and restatements, and Schedule 13D from named activist investors (free).
  4. Scans up to {{ u.scan }} companies for leadership changes, hiring, press and company events.
