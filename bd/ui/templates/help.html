How this works

BD Signals finds Chicago-area companies that show signs of needing management consulting, ranks them, and shows exactly what it found and where.

{% endblock %} {% block body %}

What to do here

  1. See what to look at this week. Open This week. Companies are listed with the most reasons to talk first. Each one says why now in a sentence.
  2. Prepare for one company. Search by name on This week (or open Companies), then open its brief. The brief is a two-minute read: the short version, what happened and when, with a link to every source. Use Print / Save as PDF or Copy as text to send it to someone.
  3. Show the market to leadership. This week has a short market summary; Reports has the Market view (which industries are heating up, signs by industry and by month).
  4. Track a company over time. On a company's brief choose Track this company. This week then shows what is new about it, and you can mark its stage and keep notes.

Words used here

Sign (signal)
One dated event found in a public source: a new executive named, a restructuring filing, a cluster of related job postings. Each one links to the page it came from.
Kinds of signs
Leadership, hiring, company events, private equity, technology, procurement, filings and financials. A company with three different kinds of signs is a stronger lead than one with three signs of the same kind.
Score
A number from 0 to {{ sc.score_max }}, worked out by plain code (no model) from the signals: how strong each kind of sign is, how recent, how trustworthy its source, and a bonus of +{{ sc.stacking_bonus_per_family|round|int }} for each extra kind that agrees (up to +{{ sc.stacking_bonus_max|round|int }}). The company page shows the sum.
Bands
Hot is {{ bands.hot }} or more, Warm {{ bands.warm }} or more, Watch {{ bands.watch }} or more. Below that a company is tracked but not listed.
Client
An existing SEI client. Clients are scored like any company but listed apart, as expansion rather than new business.
Needs facts / not scored
A company is only scanned once its basic facts (location, size, ownership) are known. Until then it waits, and an admin can fill them in.

How fresh is it

The list is refreshed by a scheduled run ({{ schedule_note }}) or when an admin presses Update my list. Every page says the date it was scored. If the scores are more than a week old, This week says so.

What it does not do

  • It does not write outreach or tell you what to say. It reports what was found.
  • It does not use LinkedIn or paywalled sites, and it only shows a source link for a page it actually retrieved.
  • It never posts to Teams from this screen.
  • It cannot see anything that is not public. A quiet company may simply be one the sources do not cover.

Where the admin work is

The small Admin link at the top right opens a separate area: updating the list, adding or looking up a company, marking clients, reviewing new companies, settings and run history. Everything you read is on the analysis side.
