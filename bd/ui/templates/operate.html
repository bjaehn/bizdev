Light refresh now

Reads every known company job board (free) and checks SEC filings not read before (a small model cost each), then re-scores. This is what runs daily except Monday; turn that on or off on Settings. It never posts.

Discover companies

Market research: finds Chicagoland companies by segment, verifies their facts, adds those that pass the fit gate (marked auto-added) and queues the rest on the Discovery page. Start with 2 segments to see the cost. Uses paid model calls.

Private equity firms

Firms come from accounts that have a PE sponsor. Sync is free. Portfolio research reads each firm's own site and adds its Chicagoland companies (same rules as Discover). Firm signals look for a new fund, an exit or an operating-partner hire; they lift the owner's portfolio companies a little. The two research buttons use paid model calls.

Open public RFPs

Searches the websites of Chicago-area agencies (City, Cook County, State, schools, transit, federal) for open consulting-type solicitations and lists them on the Open RFPs page. One search per agency group; part of Update my list. Uses paid model calls.

Fill facts from SEC filings

For companies that file with the SEC: reads revenue, public ownership and headquarters from the SEC's own data, and finds a missing CIK by an exact name match in the SEC's list of listed companies. Free (no model calls); never overwrites a stored value; every fact is logged with its source. First step of Update my list. Needs BD_USER_AGENT.

Enrich accounts

Looks up missing facts for accounts on hold and writes proposals. Review them on the Enrich review page; nothing is applied automatically. Uses paid model calls.

Scan for signals

Searches public sources for accounts that pass the fit gate. Start small (5 accounts) to see the cost. Uses paid model calls.
